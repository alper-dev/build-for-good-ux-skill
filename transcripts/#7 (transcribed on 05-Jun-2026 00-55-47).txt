Which error message placement do you choose? A toast notification, a modal in the center of the screen, or in line. This is part 7 of my series of building for good UX. So let's break it down. A toast is a message that pops up at the top or the bottom of your screen, and then it auto dismisses in a couple of seconds. Don't use toast for an important error. Think if the user looks away and they miss this, are they going to be okay? It's perfect for messages like couldn't connect to retrying. A modal takes over the center of the screen and blocks everything until the user responds. And for this reason, use this sparingly. Only when the user can't continue without addressing the issue. For example, a payment fail, so modal pops up saying your payment did not process, and it gives you an action which is a button to press to update your payment. Or a permission's error like you don't have access to this project. With a button to request access. If you're going to block the user, you have to give them a wait for. In line errors show up right next to the thing that went wrong. And as a general rule, the closer your error is to your issue, the better. So these are the ones that you'll probably use most often. For a form showing a red border for a field that needs correction. Right next to a button that they just clicked. The user clicks save and it fails. It should say, try again. right next to it.
