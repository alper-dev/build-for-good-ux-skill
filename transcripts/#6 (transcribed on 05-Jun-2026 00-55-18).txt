Nobody likes filling out forms. This is part 6 of my series of Building with Good UX. And here are 6 ways that you can reduce user frustration when filling out forms. What is don't let the user submit until everything is filled out correctly. Keep that button grayed out until all the required fields are done. But please make it really obvious what's missing. A grayed out button with no explanation is even more frustrating. Mark your required fields of users never guessing why they can't submit. 2. Is valid in line. The moment that someone leaves an email field and it's not a real email address, you need to let them know. Nothing is more frustrating than filling out a form submitting it, having it load and then if you scroll all the way up just to fix something. 3. Is show character count. If a field has a limit show how many characters are left as they're typing. Like why would you let someone write a whole paragraph and then time to find out they have to lead half of it. 4. Is refill what you can. If they're logged in don't make them type their email again. 5. Show the password requirement as they type and make sure to check off what they've done. Don't let someone submit a password and then turns out they need a capital letter. 6. When you can be forgiving with formatting. If someone types their phone number with dashes or parenthesis or nothing at all can you handle all of those and just take care of formatting to be the same in the back end.
