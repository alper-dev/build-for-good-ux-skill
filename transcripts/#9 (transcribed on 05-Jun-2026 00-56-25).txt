What happens when half your app loads and half your app doesn't? Do you wait for everything to load? This is part 9 of my build for GoodUx series. When you open an app it looks like one page, but almost nothing on that page comes from the same place. Your profile picture might come from one server, your feed might come from a completely different place. Every section of data on your page loading at different speeds. Imagine you order delivery from three different restaurants. Do you expect all of them to show up at exactly the same time, and if one of the three doesn't show up do you just throw all the food away? Of course not. You eat what showed up. Good apps work the same way where they they load what's available. When you open Instagram your stories might be ready, but your feed is still loading. Or a dashboard where the sidebar is fine, but the charts are still loading in. Some apps don't handle this well. You might see a loading screen until every single component is ready. Or if one thing breaks everything breaks and then you see an entire page error. There is a term for handling this better and it's called graceful degradation. It means your app keeps working with whatever it has. Each section loads on its own, fails on its own, and the rest of the page is still usable.
